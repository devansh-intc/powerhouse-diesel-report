What's Changed Since March 14

Same revenue, materially lower cost. Now we redeploy that headroom into top-line growth.

On March 14 we restructured the PMax setup and launched the Underspent Brands PMax: a new campaign aimed at vendor brands that were converting efficiently in the catalog but had been receiving very little ad investment. It's the single biggest change in the account this quarter.

Through April, the restructure has produced a profit-led result. Spend came down ~30%, revenue held flat at ~$610.6K, MER moved from ~21.8 to 31.45 (+44%), and net profit lifted ~8%. Same top line, lower cost, healthier account.

6. Current Observation

The campaigns are budget-constrained, not performance-constrained

The two new PMax campaigns are operating at relatively low spend levels because of historical budget caps, not because their performance has tapered.

The signal is clearest on the Underspent Brands PMax: revenue grew 59% while spend fell 31%, taking MER from ~33x to ~77x. A campaign that converts more efficiently as spend goes down is a campaign with room to take more spend.

Read: performance is ahead of investment. The fix is to invest more, in the right place.

7. Scaling Strategy & Next Steps

The Plan

Scale the Underspent Brands PMax. Hold everything else steady.

Already in motion

What we're doing

Increasing the Underspent Brands PMax budget incrementally. We have already begun this scaling, and the campaign continues to hold strong MER as more spend goes in.

Why only this one

  • It's the campaign that drove April's gains. Every other campaign either held flat or improved on lower spend.
  • Window-over-window the Underspent Brands PMax is still showing rising MER as spend changes, which is the cleanest signal in the account that headroom exists.
  • The other campaigns (Bestseller Brands PMax, All Products PMax, Branded Search) are working at their natural cap. Pushing more spend into them risks reversing the efficiency we just earned.

How we'll know it's working

  • Top-line revenue moves up, not just attributed revenue.
  • MER on the Underspent Brands PMax stays elevated as budget rises (target: hold above ~50x through the next two reporting windows).
  • Account-level MER stays at or above April's 31.45.

What we are deliberately not doing this month

  • Not aggressively scaling Bestseller Brands PMax, All Products PMax, or Branded Search. These are mature; aggressive scaling here would dilute efficiency, not grow revenue.
  • Not adding new campaigns. The discipline this month is single-lever: do one thing well.
Bottom Line

Profitability is solved. Top-line growth is the next job, and we already know which lever does it.

April proved the restructure works. The same revenue at lower cost is the result of cutting waste; the next phase is converting that earned headroom into actual top-line growth by scaling the one campaign that's been carrying the gains.
